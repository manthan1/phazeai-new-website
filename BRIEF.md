# Phaze AI redesign

## Current revision: original black theme
User direction: "The hero section is very odd, and it does not have any animation. It should match my original black color theme. Don't change that color theme."
This overrides the initial blue-and-silver direction below. All section grounds are black (#090909 or #0d0d0d), with blue/cyan confined to accents. The cube and floating task slips are removed. Centered copy sits above an animated signal network flowing through the original logo. Continuous motion is visible without scrolling; a persistent play/pause preference allows explicit opt-in for reduced-motion visitors. Offscreen and background-tab animation pauses automatically. The hero's source mark, near hub border and far signal tracks remain separate planes. The rest of the page retains its content and functional journeys.

The initial design record below is historical and is superseded wherever it conflicts with this revision.

## Eight creative topics
1. Vibe: precise, lucid, dimensional, confident; blue and silver technical studio.
2. Journey: clear offer → actual work → interactive automation example → delivery process → contact.
3. Energy: assured hero, quiet evidence, one expansive interaction, calm process, decisive close.
4. Emotion: possibility → trust → understanding → reassurance → readiness. Peak: separate tasks connect into one selected workflow.
5. Signature: a task-routing loom, with selectable scenarios carried into an email inquiry.
6. Range: premium minimal with bold type and technical diagrams.
7. Structure: distinct scenes in natural flow, one short sticky transformation.
8. Assets: existing logo; bespoke SVG illustrations, explicitly illustrative, never fake product screenshots. No generated media required.

Audience: business owners and operating teams with repetitive processes. End belief: PhazeAI can turn a specific bottleneck into a useful custom system. Primary action: Book a call.

## Feeling curve before score

Trust: sourced project names and concrete input/output descriptions.
Understanding: the selected workflow connects as the visitor advances.
Reassurance: the actual five-step delivery process is easy to inspect.
Readiness: the inquiry keeps the selected workflow.
Peak sentence: "I watched the scattered tasks turn into one connected workflow."
Tell-someone sentence: "It's the site where you choose the busywork and watch it connect."
No blank-screen silence; reading project evidence is the quiet beat before the peak.

## Grammar and fingerprint gate
Custom workflow-studio grammar: compact jump navigation; spatial offer; evidence before demo; interactive route; native process disclosures; blue inquiry desk. Bans: fake dashboards, unsourced metrics, feature-card grids, footage under copy, empty pinned travel.
Why the eight stock grammars lose: filmic hides navigation; editorial excludes the spatial opening; live surface wrongly presents services as software; continuous world invents geography; poster underplays projects; gallery forbids the opening offer; split-stage forces comparisons throughout; cutlist is too frenetic.
