# PhazeAI: Beyond ordinary

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. User asks for a dramatic scrollable background, a "crazy" client-facing capability demo; the landing page may change. Dark brand foundations retained, with cinematic cyan, lavender and amber light as authored scene transitions. This supersedes the service-homepage brief for index.html; classic.html retains that page.

Eight decisions: cinematic/spatial/unexpected; journey portal → form → fracture → arrival; energy builds through quiet sculpture into one explosion peak; curiosity → wonder → surprise → possibility; signature is a persistent chrome sculpture whose fragments form, explode and orbit under scroll; maximal spatial scene with restrained copy; one continuous world; original brand logo plus procedural geometry, no generated media or unsourced metrics.

Audience: clients seeing what an immersive website can do. Belief: their website could feel like a memorable experience. Action: Start a project, routed to the real PhazeAI email address; no automated submission.

Feeling curve BEFORE score: curiosity through a huge oblique portal; wonder through a suspended sphere of individual metal facets; surprise as the same facets unfold into an enormous helix; possibility as the camera settles on an orbital system and final invitation.
Peak: "The whole sculpture broke apart around me as I scrolled." Tell-someone sentence: "It's the site where you fly through a portal and pull a metal world apart."

Grammar: continuous world, a single fixed WebGL stage with one document spacer, waypoint navigation and a fixed copy layer. Live procedural 3D replaces pre-rendered film; camera/material/light changes are tied directly to scroll. No pinned sections, page boundaries, source-swapped films, fake interfaces, or decorative statistics. Worldflight semantics and unchanged Scroll Craft engine establish the scroll track. Native page-local rendering implements the signature.
Four waypoints weighted 1.8/1.8/2.6/1.8 viewport heights. Largest interval belongs to the fracture. During the transition into the fracture, copy yields briefly to the sculpture; this is authored visual focus, not an empty screen.

Layer contract: far stars and orbital tracks, midground metal portal, focal faceted sculpture, near floating slivers. All are real 3D geometry with perspective, occlusion and differential motion. Copy sits in a dedicated edge scrim. Opening: portal right, type left. Middle: move through portal to sphere. Peak: facets scatter and form a helix. End: fragments settle into an orbital disc behind final invitation. Mobile camera is further away and copy moves above the object. Reduced motion keeps scroll-addressable scene states with less smoothing and no ambient rotation; explicit Play permits animation. Original-scene PNG poster handles WebGL failure.

Fingerprint vs first design: changed grammar, navigation, hero, sequence, close and signature (6/6). No prior continuous-world build exists in this workspace.
